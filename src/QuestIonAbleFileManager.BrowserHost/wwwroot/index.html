QFM BrowserAgent guide

Manage your Quest

No headset app required. Your browser is an adapter to authorized device controls.

Live host controls require an explicit connection.

Install and offline help

Use your browser menu to install or add QFM to your home screen when supported.

Start the QFM connector on this computer and open its exact address for live controls. Another device's localhost points to that device, so use the connector on the computer attached to your Quest. Read setup, supported capabilities and recovery guidance.
